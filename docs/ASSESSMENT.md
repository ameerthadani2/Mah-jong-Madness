## 2. The honest headline

In two days the programme closed almost every defect the first assessment listed: the engine edge cases, the four rigging paths and the tile leak, input validation, the mock store and tamperable wallet, the missing legal surface, the English-only UI, the absent audio layer, the missing accounts and persistence, the Pro bot that lost to Club and the 30 minutes of learning content. It added Postgres-backed accounts and tables that survive a crash, server-decided purchases, telemetry, push, a full zh-TW UI and 36 bilingual lessons, all behind 906 tests. Measured against the rubric, every dimension moved, and engineering, rules and the foundation are now at or near the greenlight line.

It is still not a product anyone can use. Nothing is deployed (the deploy and web-preview jobs skip without the owner's tokens), no iOS or Android binary has ever been built, no purchase has run through a store, every sound is a synthesised beep, the legal texts are unfilled drafts, and the name collides with a live App Store title. Most of that is owner-blocked rather than engineering-blocked. The adversarial pass also found a new layer of server-side gaps that the first assessment could not reach because the server barely existed: a modified server can still pass the fairness audit on the reconnect path, one account can exhaust the global table cap, club owners have no moderation tools, and unvalidated free text can silently stop persistence. These are specific and fixable in about ten engineer-weeks; with the owner's accounts, audio and a beta, the realistic next score is about 7, and 8 needs real players.

## 3. What is genuinely good (verified on 2a151f8)

- **Rules engine.** Instant 八仙過海 and 七搶一 at the deal, mid-hand and on a kong replacement with the right payer (`packages/engine/src/game.ts:235-286`); the 河底 discard is win-only (`game.ts:421-424`); absorption rules for 天/地/人胡, 字一色, 全求人/半求人 (`scoring.ts:191-283`); maxTai before dealer 台 (`scoring.ts:143-152`). An independent scorer written from `docs/RULES.md` matched `scoreWin` on 180,000 random winning hands (0 total and 0 item mismatches); the old 30,000-hand shape check still gives 0 mismatches; 10,000 self-play hands with 30% random legal moves under random rules gave 0 claim-window, completeness or flag failures; replays reproduce `hashState` for every hand.
- **Fairness protocol on a live link.** The four 4 Nov attacks now end `failed` or `partial`: early reveal fails, stand-in abuse is `partial`, the 387-restart selective abort fails after 75 aborts ("Audit reports 1 ceremony attempts but this seat was asked to commit 76 times"), mid-hand re-deal fails. `lastDrawn` and every other view leak: 0 in 2,680 views across 8 leak classes (was 722 of 1,542). The in-app fairness copy states the server trust honestly; the coach is off at online tables.
- **Durable online tables.** On the production bundle over Postgres 16, a 4-member club match survived a SIGKILL (new process up in 580 ms, all four players back 1,053 ms after the kill, 0 draws rewound) and a SIGTERM (exit in 25 ms, all back in 1,052 ms). Own action to state p50 2 ms, p95 9 ms. Sockets end with 4401 when a session ends; membership gates club tables; HTTP, auth, socket and frame limits fire.
- **Feel and accessibility.** Audio is wired to every table event with a master switch (211 plays in 2 hands, 0 when off); call-out banners are 139-190 px; the win reveal flips the winner's hand; result sheets fit 390x844, 844x390, 667x375, 360x740 and 1024x768; 60 fps at p95 16.8 ms on the web build; 0 unnamed focusable controls across 13 screens in both languages; 745 locale keys in sync.
- **Learning.** 36 lessons in 6 tracks with 206 steps and 63 quizzes, all 63 accepted by an independent solver; 900 waits, 900 discard and 365 daily puzzles with 0 solver mismatches; the coach and the review agree on 2,007 of 2,007 moves; rating, streaks and Rush best work.
- **Money paths.** RevenueCat webhooks are authenticated, idempotent (5 parallel deliveries credited once) and refund-safe in every reordering tested; 6 forged spend shapes refused; a 999,999 local wallet is refused on spend and resynced to 0; the release bundle cannot reach the mock store; badges state +8%/+15%/+25% and deliver exactly that. ## 4. What would stop it making money (ordered by severity)

1. **Nothing is live (owner-blocked).** The deploy job and the web-preview Pages job both skip without the owner's tokens; `fly.toml` still names a placeholder app; the TestFlight workflow has never started a job; there is no Android workflow; builds without `SIGNAL_URL` point at `ws://localhost:8787` (`apps/mobile/src/config.ts:16`). Owner: Fly, Neon, `AUTH_PEPPER`, `SIGNAL_URL`, Apple and Google developer accounts. Engineering: an Android workflow and a release check on `SIGNAL_URL`.
2. **The store is not connected, and four gaps would take money for nothing once it is.** The app ignores the server's `storeEnabled` (`apps/mobile/src/monetisation/store-mode.ts:36`), so a build with RevenueCat keys against a server without the webhook secret charges and grants nothing; there is no reconciliation of missed webhooks and no support grant path (`apps/signal/src/wallet.ts:174`); an account deleted with a live subscription keeps being billed and cannot be restored to a new account (`wallet.ts:405`); 62% of the $49.99 pack and 83% of the $99.99 pack can never be spent because everything for sale costs 1,300 diamonds (`apps/mobile/src/monetisation/catalog.ts:60`). Effort: about 1 week.
3. **Brand and licence (owner).** "Mahjong Madness" is still the default and is drawn by `apps/mobile/src/components/Logo.tsx:22,25` and the share text, so a rebrand through `APP_NAME` alone would not reach players; there is no Chinese product name; the repo is public with no licence, and `docs/FAIRNESS.md` calls the engine open source.
4. **The fairness promise can still be beaten by a modified server, and the listing overclaims.** Drop-and-re-deal on the reconnect path is unbounded with every audit `ok` (150 of 150 hands; victim P(start shanten ≥ 5) 0.73 against 0.20 honest); a server rewrote 102 胡 declarations across 62 hands with every audit `ok` (`packages/net/src/client.ts:570`); phantom humans buy free restarts; one account can sit twice. `docs/store/listing/en.md:12` still says "not even the server can fix a hand". Effort: about 1.5 weeks, plus a copy change now.
5. **Clubs can be griefed and their standings gamed.** One guest on one IP fills the 500-table cap in 10.7 s because the throttles reset on reconnect (`apps/signal/src/rooms.ts:244`); any member can lock a dropped player out by filling the room's 12 peer slots (`rooms.ts:369`); any member can open a club table at 50x the house stakes (`apps/signal/src/api.ts:496`); matches against bots and multi-seat accounts count toward standings (`apps/signal/src/clubs.ts:714`); owners cannot close a table, evict or ban (`api.ts:448-509`). PokerBros-class clubs have all of these. Effort: about 2 weeks.
6. **Placeholder audio and no device run.** All 11 bundled sounds are 16 kHz synthesised beeps; the "Mandarin voice" is a musical motif and the Taiwanese set is missing (`apps/mobile/assets/audio/manifest.json`). expo-audio, haptics, safe areas, the crypto polyfill and native frame rate have never run on a phone (`docs/QA.md` entirely unchecked). Owner: commissioned audio and devices.
7. **Legal and moderation copy.** ToS and privacy have `[Company name]` and `[support email]` placeholders; the app says "Moderators review every report" (`apps/mobile/src/i18n/locales/en/legal.json:23`) while nobody reads reports; there is no user blocking and no name filter (App Review 1.2); server-side analytics ignore the player's opt-out (`wallet.ts:305`).
8. **The paid Pro tier is sold on defence it barely has.** Removing Pro's defence entirely costs +0.71 pts/hand [-0.57, 1.99]; Pro deals in as often as Club on identical tenpai states (6.5% against 6.6%). "Folds against ready players" sits in Coach Gold's copy, a "fake difficulty" refund risk.
9. **No play with strangers.** Online tables are code-only (`apps/mobile/src/i18n/locales/en/online.json:5`); 明星三缺一's core loop is instant seating with strangers.
10. **Demand is unvalidated (owner).** 0 interviews, 0 beta clubs, revenue model still an assumed 10k MAU (`docs/MONETISATION.md:85`).

## 7. Commercial view

The market facts in the first assessment stand: IGS's self-run app segment (明星三缺一 and siblings) is about NT$6.4B a year and shrinking, 神來也麻將 is modelled at about 1.4M MAU, Taiwan criminalises online gambling for players, and a mahjong game whose virtual coins rise and fall with results is rated 輔15. What changed is the product's readiness to enter it.

- **Differentiation is now demonstrable, with a caveat.** A server-hosted 16-tile table that every client audits hand by hand, plus a coach and 36 bilingual lessons, is working code. But the fairness claim must be sold as "every hand is logged and checked by your phone" rather than "cannot be rigged" until the reconnect and log-binding gaps are closed, and the store listing must change before submission.
- **The cost of a first launch has fallen.** The first assessment put a narrow first launch at about 3 months and $40-150k if contractors were paid. The engineering for that launch is now largely done; what remains is about 10 engineer-weeks of hardening (section 8) plus the owner items: name, accounts, counsel ($3-10k), audio ($5-20k), a native reviewer and devices.
- **The binding constraint has moved from engineering to the owner.** Every remaining step to a submittable build that needs money, accounts, legal sign-off, devices or players sits in `docs/OWNER_TODO.md`.

Probability-weighted outcome (estimate, not sourced): P(in stores within 12 months) about 60%, up from 50%, conditional mostly on the owner completing the Phase 1, 3, 4 and 7 rows of `docs/OWNER_TODO.md`. Given a launch with organic plus small paid acquisition, the median outcome is unchanged at 2-10k MAU and $1-5k/month gross; the likeliest good outcome is still a $5-30k/month business built on sticky private clubs, which now depends on the club moderation and quota work in section 8.

## 9. Method

Ten dimensions were each scored by an assessor who had to cite a file:line, a command and its output, or a screenshot for every claim, and was asked to cover the whole dimension, not only the old defects. Each was then re-scored by a blind adversarial challenger who re-ran the probes, read the cited code, tried what the assessor had not, and recorded every claim as confirmed, refuted, overstated or understated. The assessors reported 126 defects (34 major). Across the ten pairs the challengers recorded 242 claims as confirmed, 33 overstated, 23 understated and 0 refuted, and added 63 defects the assessors missed, 16 of them major. Every pair agreed within one point, so each dimension takes the mean. Six of the challengers' major findings were then checked in the code by the synthesiser (section 1).

What was run: the engine, net, signal and mobile suites (signal also against a private Postgres 16 cluster); the old 4 Nov probes re-pointed at the current code; new probes including an independent 台 scorer (180,000 hands), 10,000 self-play hands with random legal moves, 12 fairness attacks against the real host and client, a production-bundle server over Postgres put through SIGKILL, SIGTERM and a hung database, 200-table load, 21,600 hostile HTTP requests, 14,000 fuzzed socket frames, bot ladders of up to 4,000 paired hands on three fresh seed sets, an exact deal-in enumeration for the defence puzzles, and Playwright runs of the web build at five viewports in both languages. Nothing was run on a phone, and nothing was run against a deployed server, because neither exists yet.

Scratch artifacts are under the session scratchpad (`assess2/<dimension>/` and `assess2/<dimension>-challenge/`); the full structured results of all twenty agents are in `assess2/results.json`. They are not committed.
