# Store compliance

What the app does today, measured against the store rules, and the answers to give at submission. Apple rules were checked against the App Review Guidelines and the Developer Program License Agreement (November 2026). Google Play's policy text and the IARC questionnaire could not be read in that research session: every Play and IARC item below is marked **verify** and must be checked against the live pages before submission. Nothing here is legal advice; the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy in the app are DRAFTS pending counsel review.

## What exists in the app

| Surface | Where | State |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Store | `apps/mobile/src/monetisation/` | RevenueCat (`react-native-purchases`) when `EXPO_PUBLIC_REVENUECAT_IOS_KEY` / `_ANDROID_KEY` is set; otherwise the Shop says "Store unavailable on this build" and grants nothing. The Shop also says "Store unavailable" and sells nothing for money when the server reports `storeEnabled: false` (no webhook secret), so a purchase can never be charged and then not granted. The mock store exists only in development builds with `EXPO_PUBLIC_STORE_MODE=mock`. Sandbox purchases are granted only to accounts on the server's tester list (`TESTER_USER_IDS`). |
| Entitlements | server `entitlements` table, read by the app from `GET /api/me` | The app never grants anything itself; a tampered local cache is overwritten on the next sync. Expiry is judged by the server clock. |
| Restore Purchases | Shop, under the products | Calls the store's restore, asks the server to reconcile with the store (`POST /api/wallet/sync`, which applies anything a webhook missed), then re-reads `/api/me`. If the server cannot check the purchases, the Shop says so instead of "Purchases restored". Subscriptions come back on any account signed into the same store account (also after the old account was deleted), for the plans bought on that store; diamonds and looks belong to the game account and the Shop says so. |
| Catalogue | `catalog.ts`, `docs/MONETISATION.md` | Diamond packs and two subscriptions through the store: Coach Gold and The Four Winds Club (monthly and yearly). Cosmetics bought with diamonds only: 12 table looks, 10 tile backs, 12 avatar frames, 4 emote packs, 4 win celebrations and 4 sets (13,900 diamonds in all, so every pack can be spent in full). All drawn in code, with no coins, chips or money imagery. No loot boxes, no randomised items, nothing that changes play. |
| "No monetary value" | Shop, Clubs list, club standings, Settings | "Diamonds and points have no monetary value and cannot be redeemed or transferred." Also in the Terms, section 4. |
| Terms of Service, Privacy Policy | Settings → Legal, the first-run age gate, the Shop | Bundled DRAFT text (en, zh-TW) under a "DRAFT — pending counsel review" banner, or the published page in the browser when `EXPO_PUBLIC_TERMS_URL` / `EXPO_PUBLIC_PRIVACY_URL` is set (https only). |
| Age gate | first run (fresh install), else before the first visit to the Shop or Clubs | Birth year only, asked neutrally ("What year were you born?", without the limit); under **17** sees a polite blocked screen with the year they may return. With no birthday the gate assumes the latest one, so a player counts as 17 from 1 January of the year they turn 18. The answer is stored on the device and sent with `PATCH /api/me` (`users.birth_year`) once there is a session. The limit is `AGE_LIMIT` in `apps/mobile/src/legal/age.ts`; raise it if the store rating comes out higher. The web e2e (`apps/mobile/e2e/play-hand.cjs`) seeds a saved profile, so it runs as an older install: Home is not gated and the run never opens the Shop or Clubs. |
| Account deletion | Settings → Account → Delete account | `DELETE /api/me` (soft delete, scrub after 30 days, purchase records kept). The confirmation says diamonds and looks are lost; with a live subscription it shows the plan and its renewal date, links to the App Store or Play subscription page ("Manage subscription"), and needs an explicit tick that the store keeps billing until the player cancels there. A Restore on a new account moves the subscription across. |
| Report player | club standings (and the table once wired) | `ReportPlayerSheet` → `POST /api/reports` with a fixed reason list and an optional note. Reports are reviewed by a person; there is no automated detection yet. |
| Clubs | free | Points-only standings with seasons ("Start new season" for owners archives the season and keeps all-time totals). No chips, no stakes, no settlement features, no transfers between players. |

The iOS privacy manifest is generated by `app.config.js` (`ios.privacyManifests`): email, user id, device id, purchase history, user content (report text, display names), other data (birth year) and gameplay content (hands, results, standings, puzzle answers) linked to the user; product interaction (analytics) and crash data not linked. Server-side analytics (`purchase_credited`) are sent only for players whose analytics choice, sent with `PATCH /api/me`, is yes. Not yet in the app (owner or other tracks): a web page or form for account deletion requests (Play requires one), Apple's Declared Age Range API, the support email in the legal texts, counsel-approved legal texts.

## Age rating answers

**Apple questionnaire.** Simulated Gambling: **Frequent/Intense**. Contests: None (no prizes). Unrestricted web access: No. User-generated content: Yes (display names, club names and descriptions; no free chat). Messaging and chat: No free text (fixed emotes and quick phrases only). In-app purchases: Yes. Advertising: No. Expect the highest band Apple assigns for frequent simulated gambling (17+ under the older scale; check how the current bands map before submission). The in-app gate must be at least the rating.

**Google Play / IARC (verify).** Simulated gambling: Yes. Real-money gambling: No. Users interact: Yes (shared tables and clubs, no free chat). Shares location: No. Digital purchases: Yes. Expected outcome: a teen-or-higher rating with a "Simulated Gambling" descriptor that varies by region; Taiwan's 遊戲軟體分級 is planned as 輔15 (counsel to confirm). The in-app gate stays at 17 whatever the regional rating.

## Google Play Data Safety answers (verify against the live form)

Collected and linked to the account; not sold; not shared beyond service providers (hosting, database, RevenueCat for purchases, and crash reporting / analytics only when the build carries the keys and the player's toggle is on). Data is encrypted in transit (https / wss). Users can delete their account in the app; Play also needs a web deletion URL (owner).

| Play category | Data | Required? | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Personal info → Name | display name (chosen by the player) | Required for online play | App functionality |
| Personal info → Email address | only when Sign in with Apple/Google shares a verified email | Optional | Account management |
| Personal info → User IDs | server account id; Apple/Google subject id when linked | Required online | App functionality, account management |
| Personal info → Other info | birth year | Required | Legal compliance (age gate) |
| Financial info → Purchase history | product, transaction id, price, currency (from the store via RevenueCat) | Only for buyers | App functionality (delivering purchases) |
| App activity → In-app actions / other | tables joined, hands (shuffle log), results, standings, reports filed | Required online | App functionality, fraud prevention |
| App activity → Other user-generated content | the free-text note on a player report (`reports.details`, up to 1,000 characters) and display names (`users.display_name`, match results), stored with the user id | Required online (names); optional (report notes) | App functionality (moderation, standings) |
| App info and performance → Crash logs, diagnostics | crash data only while the player's "Send crash reports" toggle is on (it also stops native crash reports); usage analytics only while "Share usage statistics" is on; neither exists in a build without the keys | Optional | Analytics, app functionality |
| Device or other IDs | random device id made by the app, platform, app build | Required online | App functionality |

Not collected: location, contacts, photos, messages, health, payment card details (the store handles payment), advertising id.

**Apple App Privacy ("nutrition label").** Data linked to you: Contact info (email, only via Sign in), Identifiers (user id, device id), Purchases, User content (report text, display names), Gameplay content (hands, results, standings, puzzle answers), Other data (birth year). Data not linked to you: Usage data (product interaction, only when analytics are on; app events are anonymous), Diagnostics (crash data, only while the crash toggle is on, native crashes included). Tracking: No.

## App Review notes (paste at submission, adjusted to the build)

> This is a Taiwanese 16-tile mahjong game with simulated gambling and no real-money play. Diamonds and points have no monetary value and cannot be redeemed or transferred; there is no cash-out, no prizes and no way to move anything between players. Diamonds buy only cosmetic looks (table felts, tile backs, avatar frames, emotes, win celebrations). Coach Gold and The Four Winds Club are auto-renewing subscriptions: Coach Gold unlocks stronger practice bots and unlimited Puzzle Rush; The Four Winds Club adds unlimited hand reviews and the right to create and run clubs. Nothing sold changes the tiles dealt, what a player can see at the table, or the clock. Joining and playing in clubs is free, with points-only standings. Purchases are restored from the Shop ("Restore purchases"). Players must be 17 or older: the app asks for a birth year on first launch, without saying which years are accepted. Accounts can be deleted in Settings → Account. Players can be reported from club standings; reports are reviewed by a person. Every online hand is logged and checked on the player's phone: the shuffle is seeded by all seats and the app replays and checks the hand afterwards ("Fair play"). Demo account: [reviewer account details].
