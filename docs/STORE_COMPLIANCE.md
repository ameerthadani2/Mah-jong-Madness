# Store compliance

What the app does today, measured against the store rules, and the answers to give at submission. Apple rules were checked against the App Review Guidelines and the Developer Program License Agreement (November 2026). Google Play's policy text and the IARC questionnaire could not be read in that research session: every Play and IARC item below is marked **verify** and must be checked against the live pages before submission. Nothing here is legal advice; the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy in the app are DRAFTS pending counsel review.

## What exists in the app

| Surface | Where | State |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Store | `apps/mobile/src/monetisation/` | RevenueCat (`react-native-purchases`) when `EXPO_PUBLIC_REVENUECAT_IOS_KEY` / `_ANDROID_KEY` is set; otherwise the Shop says "Store unavailable on this build" and grants nothing. The Shop also says "Store unavailable" and sells nothing for money when the server reports `storeEnabled: false` (no webhook secret), so a purchase can never be charged and then not granted. The mock store exists only in development builds with `EXPO_PUBLIC_STORE_MODE=mock`. Sandbox purchases are granted only to accounts on the server's tester list (`TESTER_USER_IDS`); App Review buys in the sandbox, so the reviewer's account must be on that list before submission (see "App Review notes"). |
| Entitlements | server `entitlements` table, read by the app from `GET /api/me` | The app never grants anything itself; a tampered local cache is overwritten on the next sync. Expiry is judged by the server clock. |
| Restore Purchases | Shop, under the products | Calls the store's restore, asks the server to reconcile with the store (`POST /api/wallet/sync`, which applies anything a webhook missed), then re-reads `/api/me`. If the server cannot check the purchases, the Shop says so instead of "Purchases restored". Subscriptions come back on any account signed into the same store account (also after the old account was deleted), for the plans bought on that store; diamonds and looks belong to the game account and the Shop says so. |
| Catalogue | `catalog.ts`, `docs/MONETISATION.md` | Diamond packs and two subscriptions through the store: Coach Gold and The Four Winds Club (monthly and yearly). Cosmetics bought with diamonds only: 12 table looks, 10 tile backs, 12 avatar frames, 4 emote packs, 4 win celebrations and 4 sets (13,900 diamonds in all, so every pack can be spent in full). All drawn in code, with no coins, chips or money imagery. No loot boxes, no randomised items, nothing that changes play. |
| "No monetary value" | Shop, Clubs list, club standings, Settings | "Diamonds and points have no monetary value and cannot be redeemed or transferred." Also in the Terms, section 4. |
| Terms of Service, Privacy Policy | Settings → Legal, the first-run age gate, the Shop | Bundled DRAFT text (en, zh-TW) under a "DRAFT — pending counsel review" banner, or the published page in the browser when `EXPO_PUBLIC_TERMS_URL` / `EXPO_PUBLIC_PRIVACY_URL` is set (https only). |
| Age gate | first run (fresh install), else before the first visit to the Shop or Clubs | Birth year only, asked neutrally ("What year were you born?", without the limit); under **18** sees a polite blocked screen with the year they may return. With no birthday the gate assumes the latest one, so a player counts as 18 from 1 January of the year they turn 19. The answer is stored on the device and sent with `PATCH /api/me` (`users.birth_year`) once there is a session, and only when the server has none (the year is write-once there). The limit is `AGE_LIMIT` in `apps/mobile/src/legal/age.ts`, and the server applies the same age to quick match (`QUICK_MATCH_MIN_AGE` in `apps/signal/src/matchmaking.ts`): both are 18, the band the App Store gives frequent simulated gambling. Right after the age question the app asks once whether it may send usage statistics and crash reports; nothing is sent to either service before the player agrees (see "Usage statistics and crash reports" below). The web e2e (`apps/mobile/e2e/play-hand.cjs`) seeds a saved profile, so it runs as an older install: Home is not gated and the run never opens the Shop or Clubs. |
| Account deletion | Settings → Account → Delete account | `DELETE /api/me`. At once: the name, avatar, email, birth year, language, sign-in methods, devices (with their push tokens), sessions and block list go, and "Deleted player" replaces the name in club member lists, finished club seasons and finished match records, and inside the notes of reports filed by or about the account. After 30 days the account row and what hangs from it (puzzle history, plans and looks, moderation state) are erased and the account id is cleared from the match records. What stays is listed under "What is kept after an account is deleted". The confirmation says diamonds and looks are lost; with a live subscription it shows the plan and its renewal date, links to the App Store or Play subscription page ("Manage subscription"), and needs an explicit tick that the store keeps billing until the player cancels there. A Restore on a new account moves the subscription across. |
| Report and block | the table (long press on a seat) and club standings | `ReportPlayerSheet` → `POST /api/reports` with a fixed reason list and an optional note, and Block / Unblock through `/api/blocks`. A blocked player's emotes and quick phrases are hidden and quick match never seats the two at one table. Reports are reviewed by a person, who can dismiss, warn, mute (emotes and phrases dropped everywhere) or ban (the account and its devices); display names and club names are checked against a blocklist when they are set. There is no automated detection of cheating. |
| Quick match | Home → Quick match | Seats the player with strangers (bots take the empty seats after a short wait, 20 seconds by default). The server refuses an account that is banned, has no birth year on file, or whose year makes it younger than 18. No free chat: fixed emotes and quick phrases only. |
| Clubs | free to join and play; creating and running one needs The Four Winds Club (once the store is live) | Points-only standings with seasons ("Start new season" for owners archives the season and keeps all-time totals). No chips, no stakes, no settlement features, no transfers between players. |

The iOS privacy manifest is generated by `app.config.js` (`ios.privacyManifests`): email, user id, device id, purchase history (app functionality and analytics), user content (report text, display names), other data (birth year), gameplay content (hands, results, standings, puzzle answers) and product interaction (analytics) linked to the user; crash data not linked. Usage data is declared as linked because the server's own events (`purchase_credited`, `report_filed`) are sent under the account id; they are sent only for players who agreed to usage statistics (the choice reaches the server with `PATCH /api/me`). Not yet in the app (owner or other tracks): a web page or form for account deletion requests (Play requires one), Apple's Declared Age Range API, the support email in the legal texts, counsel-approved legal texts.

## Usage statistics and crash reports

Both are opt-in. The app asks once, straight after the age question, and a player who has not answered, or said no, sends nothing: no app event, no crash report (native crashes included), and the server sends no event of its own about that account. The two switches stay in Settings → Privacy, where either can be turned on or off at any time. A build without the keys has neither service and hides the switches. Whether an opt-in prompt is enough in every launch market is still a question for counsel (`docs/OWNER_TODO.md`).

## What is kept after an account is deleted

| Record | What stays | For how long |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Purchases, the diamond ledger, store notifications | product, transaction id, price, currency, amounts and times, under the account's random id | The server never deletes them; the period tax and accounting law requires is for counsel to set (the Privacy Policy says "as long as tax and accounting law requires"). |
| Finished matches | seats, scores and hands won; the seat reads "Deleted player" from the moment of deletion, and its account id is cleared at the 30-day purge | Kept; nothing in the row leads to a person after the purge. |
| Finished club seasons | the points of the row, named "Deleted player", under the account's random id | Until the club drops the season (a club keeps its last 24) or is deleted. |
| Reports filed by or about the account | reason, time, table code, outcome and the note, with the account's name taken out of the note, under the random account ids | Kept as moderation records; the server never deletes them. |
| Club bans of the account | "Deleted player" and the random id | While the club exists. |

Everything else goes: at once (name, avatar, email, birth year, language, sign-in methods, devices and push tokens, sessions, block list, club membership rows renamed "Deleted player") or with the purge 30 days later (the account row, puzzle history, plans and looks, mute and ban state, the club membership rows). A table still in play keeps the seat's name on screen until it ends. An account merged into another (a guest that signed in to an existing account) is not a deletion: its matches and season rows move to the surviving account. The in-app Privacy Policy (section 6) and Terms (section 3) must say the same; they are DRAFTS.

## Age rating answers

**Apple questionnaire.** Simulated Gambling: **Frequent/Intense**. Contests: None (no prizes). Unrestricted web access: No. User-generated content: Yes (display names and club names; no free chat). Messaging and chat: No free text (fixed emotes and quick phrases only). In-app purchases: Yes. Advertising: No. Players meet strangers in quick match (bots fill empty seats); the server refuses it without a birth year of 18 or over. Expect **18+**: Apple's current scale (4+, 9+, 13+, 16+, 18+) puts frequent simulated gambling at 18+, and shows 17+ only on devices running an operating system older than version 26. The in-app gate (18) must be at least the rating; check the questionnaire's result at submission.

**Google Play / IARC (verify).** Simulated gambling: Yes. Real-money gambling: No. Users interact: Yes (shared tables, clubs and quick match with strangers; no free chat). Shares location: No. Digital purchases: Yes. Expected outcome: a teen-or-higher rating with a "Simulated Gambling" descriptor that varies by region; Taiwan's 遊戲軟體分級 is planned as 輔15 (counsel to confirm). The in-app gate stays at 18 whatever the regional rating.

## Google Play Data Safety answers (verify against the live form)

Collected and linked to the account; not sold; not shared beyond service providers (hosting, database, RevenueCat for purchases, Expo's push service, which passes notifications to Apple and Google, and crash reporting / analytics only when the build carries the keys and the player has agreed). Data is encrypted in transit (https / wss). Users can delete their account in the app; Play also needs a web deletion URL (owner).

| Play category | Data | Required? | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Personal info → Name | display name (chosen by the player) | Required for online play | App functionality |
| Personal info → Email address | only when Sign in with Apple/Google shares a verified email | Optional | Account management |
| Personal info → User IDs | server account id; Apple/Google subject id when linked | Required online | App functionality, account management |
| Personal info → Other info | birth year | Required | Legal compliance (age gate) |
| Financial info → Purchase history | product, transaction id, price, currency (from the store via RevenueCat) | Only for buyers | App functionality (delivering purchases); analytics, only for a buyer who agreed to usage statistics (the server's `purchase_credited` event carries the account id and the product) |
| App activity → In-app actions / other | tables joined, hands (shuffle log), results, standings, reports filed, the players the account has blocked, the usage-statistics choice | Required online | App functionality, fraud prevention |
| App activity → App interactions | usage events, only after the player agrees: the app's own events carry no account id; the server's (`purchase_credited`, `report_filed`) carry the account id | Optional | Analytics |
| App activity → Other user-generated content | the free-text note on a player report (`reports.details`, up to 1,000 characters) and display names (`users.display_name`, match results), stored with the user id | Required online (names); optional (report notes) | App functionality (moderation, standings) |
| App info and performance → Crash logs, diagnostics | crash data only after the player agrees and while "Send crash reports" is on (off also stops native crash reports); it carries no account id; not in a build without the keys | Optional | App functionality |
| Device or other IDs | random device id made by the app, platform, app build; a push notification token, only if the player allows notifications (registered when they join or create a club; cleared when they sign out on that device, switch notifications off or delete the account) | Required online (device id); optional (push token) | App functionality |

Not collected: location, contacts, photos, messages, health, payment card details (the store handles payment), advertising id.

**Apple App Privacy ("nutrition label").** Data linked to you: Contact info (email, only via Sign in), Identifiers (user id, device id, and the push token if notifications are allowed), Purchases (app functionality, and analytics once you agree to usage statistics), User content (report text, display names), Gameplay content (hands, results, standings, puzzle answers), Other data (birth year), Usage data (product interaction, only after you agree: the server's purchase and report events carry your account id; the app's own events do not). Data not linked to you: Diagnostics (crash data, only after you agree and while the crash switch is on, native crashes included). Tracking: No.

## App Review notes (paste at submission, adjusted to the build)

> This is a Taiwanese 16-tile mahjong game with simulated gambling and no real-money play. Diamonds and points have no monetary value and cannot be redeemed or transferred; there is no cash-out, no prizes and no way to move anything between players. Diamonds buy only cosmetic looks (table felts, tile backs, avatar frames, emotes, win celebrations). Coach Gold and The Four Winds Club are auto-renewing subscriptions: Coach Gold unlocks stronger practice bots and unlimited Puzzle Rush; The Four Winds Club adds unlimited hand reviews and the right to create and run clubs. Nothing sold changes the tiles dealt, what a player can see at the table, or the clock. Joining and playing in clubs is free, with points-only standings. Purchases are restored from the Shop ("Restore purchases"). Players must be 18 or older: the app asks for a birth year on first launch, without saying which years are accepted, and then asks once whether usage statistics and crash reports may be sent (nothing is sent without a yes). Quick match seats players with strangers; there is no free chat, only fixed emotes and phrases. Players can be reported and blocked from the table (long press on a seat) or from club standings; reports are reviewed by a person, who can warn, mute or ban. Accounts can be deleted in Settings → Account. On iOS, Sign in with Apple is offered wherever Google sign-in is. Every online hand is logged and checked on the player's phone: the shuffle is seeded by all seats and the app replays and checks the hand afterwards ("Fair play"). To test purchases and club creation, sign in in Settings → Account with the demo account below: sandbox purchases are granted only to accounts on our tester list, and this account is on it (a purchase made on a fresh guest account is recorded and grants nothing). Demo account: [sign-in method, e-mail and password of the demo account].

The demo account has to exist before the notes are true. One of two ways (docs/OWNER_TODO.md, step 8): (a) on a test device make an account, link it to a demo Apple or Google identity made for the purpose, read its Player id in Settings → Account, add that id to the server's `TESTER_USER_IDS`, and give App Review the identity's e-mail and password; or (b) if App Review sends the Player id of the account it used, add that id to `TESTER_USER_IDS` and ask them to buy again. Without one of these every purchase the reviewer makes is recorded as `ignored_sandbox`, nothing arrives and club creation stays refused.
